Sharing my Baby with the World

It’s time to announce my baby to the world….

…..the baby that is my Google Maps lists

I’ve been working away at these for years now and I think it’s time you get to use them too. You can open these lists and save them all to your Google Maps account. Then you’ll see a plethora of inspiration for accomodation, dining, shopping, and much more.

THERE’S ABSOLUTELY ZERO SPONSORED CONTENT IN HERE!

Each list is split up into these categories;

  • Cafes, Restaurants, & Bars
  • Accomodation
  • Shopping
  • Groceries & Markets
  • Beauty & Self Care
  • Nature & Cities to Visit
  • Activities
  • Physical Exercise
  • Health & Medical

Simply open the link and hit "save"

If you have suggestions of places to add or remove from the lists please send them to me at goodjujutimes@gmail.com or message me on Instagram @goodjujutimes

Disclaimer: some of these are cheap and cheerful, others are very high end. I haven’t personally been to all these places. In some cases I’ve only seen them on social media, so please do some research before committing to make sure it’s genuinely good and not a victim of virality and faux hype.

Happy exploring!
